7.1 Two different small parameters

两个不同的小参数

The wall Mach number M=∣R˙∣/cM=|\dot R|/cM=∣R˙∣/c measures how fast the interface moves relative to sound. For harmonic forcing, kR=ωR/ckR=\omega R/ckR=ωR/c compares bubble size with acoustic wavelength. A low wall speed does not automatically make a very high-frequency forcing field spatially uniform. Likewise, a bubble may spend most of its life at small MMM but leave that regime during its final collapse. [S413, S409]

界面Mach数M=∣R˙∣/cM=|\dot R|/cM=∣R˙∣/c衡量界面运动相对于声速的快慢。对简谐激励，kR=ωR/ckR=\omega R/ckR=ωR/c比较气泡尺寸与声波波长。界面速度较低，并不自动保证高频激励场在空间上均匀。同样，一个气泡可能在大部分寿命中保持较小MMM，却在塌缩末期离开这一范围。[S413, S409]

A radial compressible equation can include outgoing acoustic energy while still treating the bubble as spherical. It does not thereby become a three-dimensional shock-resolving simulation. Model dimensionality, thermodynamic closure, and compressibility order are separate choices.

可压缩径向方程可以在保持球形假设的同时计入向外传播的声能，但这并不使它成为三维激波解析仿真。模型维度、热力学闭合和可压缩性近似阶次，是彼此不同的选择。

7.2 Keller–Miksis with constant ambient pressure

For the numerical laboratory we deliberately use a constant far-field pressure p0p_0p0​. One common first-order compressible form is

Here pL=pg+pv−2σ/R−4μR˙/Rp_L=p_g+p_v-2\sigma/R-4\mu\dot R/RpL​=pg​+pv​−2σ/R−4μR˙/R is the liquid-side interfacial pressure, not just gas pressure. As c→∞c\to\inftyc→∞, equation (7.1) reduces to the incompressible radial equation. This limiting check is implemented in the supplied code. [S413]

这里pL=pg+pv−2σ/R−4μR˙/Rp_L=p_g+p_v-2\sigma/R-4\mu\dot R/RpL​=pg​+pv​−2σ/R−4μR˙/R是界面液侧压力，而不只是气体压力。当c→∞c\to\inftyc→∞时，式(7.1)退化为不可压缩径向方程。配套代码实际执行了这一极限检查。[S413]

Published acoustically forced forms depend on the convention for the imposed incident pressure, its evaluation time, and retained asymptotic terms. The original Keller–Miksis derivation explicitly contains an incident-field timing convention. Do not take a forcing term from one convention and a pressure derivative from another. The laboratory avoids that ambiguity by implementing free ring-down at constant p0p_0p0​; extending it to a measured acoustic waveform requires a deliberate derivation. [S413]

文献中受声激励形式的差异，与入射压力的定义、求值时刻及保留的渐近项有关。Keller–Miksis原始推导明确包含入射场的时间约定。不能从一种约定中取激励项，再从另一种约定中取压力导数。配套实验通过实现恒定p0p_0p0​下的自由衰减振荡来避免这种混用；要扩展至实测声压波形，必须重新明确推导约定。[S413]

7.3 Solving the implicit acceleration correctly

正确求解隐式加速度

Let U=R˙U=\dot RU=R˙. For constant pvp_vpv​, constant σ\sigmaσ, and the polytropic gas law, differentiation gives

令U=R˙U=\dot RU=R˙。当pvp_vpv​和σ\sigmaσ恒定，且气体满足多方定律时，求导得到

dpLdt=−3κpgUR+2σUR2+4μU2R2−4μRU˙.(7.2)\frac{dp_L}{dt}=-\frac{3\kappa p_gU}{R} +\frac{2\sigma U}{R^2}+\frac{4\mu U^2}{R^2} -\frac{4\mu}{R}\dot U. \tag{7.2}dtdpL​​=−R3κpg​U​+R22σU​+R24μU2​−R4μ​U˙.(7.2)

The derivative contains the unknown acceleration. Omitting the last term is not the same model. Define DpD_pDp​ as the first three terms in (7.2); then the explicit acceleration used in the code is

该导数中包含未知加速度。漏掉最后一项，就不再是同一个模型。将式(7.2)前三项记作DpD_pDp​，配套代码采用的显式加速度为

U˙=(1+U/c)(pL−p0)/ρ+RDp/(ρc)−32(1−U/(3c))U2R(1−U/c)+4μ/(ρc).(7.3)\dot U=\frac{ (1+U/c)(p_L-p_0)/\rho+RD_p/(\rho c) -\tfrac32(1-U/(3c))U^2} {R(1-U/c)+4\mu/(\rho c)}. \tag{7.3}U˙=R(1−U/c)+4μ/(ρc)(1+U/c)(pL​−p0​)/ρ+RDp​/(ρc)−23​(1−U/(3c))U2​.(7.3)

This is a useful example of a general computational principle: when a constitutive quantity depends on velocity, its time derivative may contain acceleration. Symbolically rearrange the equation before passing it to an explicit right-hand-side function.

这说明了一个普遍计算原则：当某个本构量依赖速度时，其时间导数可能包含加速度。在将方程交给显式右端函数之前，应先完成符号整理。

7.5 What the far-field pressure means

远场压力究竟表示什么

For a compact spherical source in a weakly nonlinear or linear far-field propagation approximation, the monopole pressure is

在紧致球形声源以及弱非线性或线性远场传播近似下，单极子声压为

p′(r,t)≃ρ4πrV¨(t−r/c)=ρr[R2R¨+2RR˙2]t−r/c.(7.7)p'(r,t)\simeq\frac{\rho}{4\pi r}\ddot V(t-r/c) =\frac{\rho}{r}\left[R^2\ddot R+2R\dot R^2\right]_{t-r/c}. \tag{7.7}p′(r,t)≃4πrρ​V¨(t−r/c)=rρ​[R2R¨+2RR˙2]t−r/c​.(7.7)

The observation distance rrr is measured from the bubble center and must be in a suitable radiation region. A local wall-impact pressure cannot be obtained by putting a tiny rrr into this far-field formula. Nor does equation (7.7) resolve shock steepening, attenuation, reflection, sensor averaging, or nonspherical jets.

观测距离rrr从气泡中心量起，且必须位于适当的辐射区域。不能通过在远场公式中代入极小rrr来获得局部壁面冲击压力。式(7.7)也不解析激波陡化、衰减、反射、传感器平均效应或非球形射流。

For a freely outgoing, approximately linear spherical wave, an acoustic-energy estimate is

对于自由向外传播、近似线性的球面波，声能估计为

Eac≃4πr2ρc∫p′(r,t)2 dt.(7.8)E_{\mathrm{ac}}\simeq\frac{4\pi r^2}{\rho c}\int p'(r,t)^2\,dt. \tag{7.8}Eac​≃ρc4πr2​∫p′(r,t)2dt.(7.8)

Use this only with its propagation assumptions. An arbitrary near-field pressure trace may include reactive rather than radiated energy. Your laser-bubble source [S409] is useful precisely because it distinguishes liquid compression, acoustic emission, and other energy channels rather than equating them all to pVpVpV.

使用该式时必须保留其传播假设。任意近场压力历程可能包含反应性储能，而不是纯辐射能量。你提供的激光气泡文献[S409]的重要价值，正是在于区分液体压缩、声发射及其他能量通道，而不是把它们都等同于pVpVpV。

7.6 Stop before you manufacture a result

在制造伪结果之前停止

A solver can integrate beyond the validity of its model. The supplied laboratory uses a conservative teaching guard at ∣U∣/c=0.10|U|/c=0.10∣U∣/c=0.10 and a finite minimum radius. The value 0.10 is a chosen warning threshold, not a universal accuracy boundary for Keller–Miksis. A stopped calculation is preferable to reporting a spurious pressure spike generated by an invalid extrapolation.

求解器可能在模型适用范围之外继续积分。配套数值实验在∣U∣/c=0.10|U|/c=0.10∣U∣/c=0.10处设置保守教学保护，并设置有限最小半径。0.10是人为选择的警戒阈值，不是Keller–Miksis模型普适的精度分界线。与其报告无效外推产生的虚假压力尖峰，不如及时停止计算。
