4 · One bubble → 3–5 bubbles → finite arrays

4 · 单泡 → 三至五泡 → 有限阵列

First retain a fixed source-energy budget and define the receiver, pressure area/time window and jet tracking mass. Compare isolated, delayed, interacting and coalescing bubbles. An identical number of stamp sites does not establish an interacting bubble population. Test edge effects, activation delays, optical shielding and phase/heat competition before a uniform-fluid approximation.

先保持固定热源能量预算，并定义接收体、压力面积／时窗及射流追踪质量。比较孤立、延迟、相互作用及聚并气泡。相同数量的印章位点不能确立相互作用泡群。在采用均匀流体近似前，应检验边缘效应、激活延迟、光学屏蔽及相变／热量竞争。

A weak linear population model can test phase and far-field superposition. Strong collapse, jets and attainable pressure maxima require nonlinear source histories, finite species inventory, compressibility and actual boundaries. Compare fixed-energy amplification at the release interface with local numerical peaks; use conservation and spatial/time refinement.

弱线性泡群模型可检验相位与远场叠加。强塌缩、射流及可达最高压力需要非线性源历程、有限组分存量、可压缩性与实际边界。应将固定能量下释放界面的放大与局部数值峰值比较，并检查守恒及空间／时间细化。

5 · Compare several benefits together

5 · 同时比较多种优点

Keep absorbed energy, onset variability, heated ink volume/temperature, phase conversion/reset, jet mass/diameter/velocity, delivered impulse, intact transfer, placement and reuse. Report gains and penalties separately; a reduced activation threshold need not increase useful impulse, and compliant confinement can improve repeatability while reducing ejection.

保留吸收能量、起始差异、墨片受热体积／温度、相变转换／复位、射流质量／直径／速度、传递冲量、完整转印、定位及复用。分别报告收益与代价；激活阈值降低未必增加有用冲量，柔顺约束也可能在降低喷出时改善重复性。

Report the highest verified single-shot pressure/velocity under its operating constraints, then the repeatable intact-transfer envelope under separately stated acceptance criteria. A pressure peak, jet tip speed and film load are different outputs. This exploration map supplies tests and opportunities; it does not claim a solved project maximum.

在其操作约束下报告最高已核验单次压力／速度，再按另行声明的接受标准报告可重复完整转印范围。压力峰值、射流尖端速度与薄膜载荷是不同输出。本探索图提供核验与机会，不声称项目最高值已解。
