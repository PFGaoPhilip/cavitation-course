What “close the chain” means

“完成理论链”的含义

Each stage states its governing equations, closure laws, initial/boundary data, conserved quantities and output definitions. The analytic controls are solved and numerically checked. State and budgets are passed to the next stage. A specified nonlinear free-surface geometry still requires a spatial solution; a table of models is not an experimental verification or a universal closed-form maximum.

每阶段明确支配方程、闭合规律、初始／边界数据、守恒量及输出定义。解析对照已求解并数值核验，状态与预算传到下一阶段。指定非线性自由表面几何仍需空间求解；模型表格不是实验验证，也不是通用闭式最高值。

The substantial arrangement follows the earlier pressure-limit chapter: start with a soluble control, derive the refinement, calculate its implications, identify its transition, and end with a multi-part problem. Original chapters and accepted solutions are retained. Every displayed formula is followed by its own physical variable map, and every reference answer begins with the original formulas.

完整安排参考早期压力极限章节：从可解对照开始，推导改进，计算其含义，确定阶段转换，最后以多部分综合题收尾。原章节及已接受解答保留。每个显示公式后都有相应物理变量图，每个参考答案先列原始公式。

After J04: specialize the supplied source and compare architectures

J04 之后：特化给定源并比较结构

L01 becomes the bridge to optical deposition, heat flow and finite PFC phase inventory. The subsequent project route keeps PFC in a finite water-droplet stamp, the primary PFC-containing liquid layer below film/PVC, and PFC embedded in hydrogel. Both direct liquid-to-film and intact-PVC-mediated loading remain. Compare mechanisms and both output envelopes without forced early selection.

L01 成为连接光学沉积、热流与有限 PFC 相存量的桥梁。后续项目路线保留有限水滴印章中的 PFC、主要薄膜／PVC 下含 PFC 液层及水凝胶嵌入 PFC。液体直接作用于薄膜及完整 PVC 介导载荷均保留。比较机制及两类输出范围，不强制提前选一个分支。

Harmonic acoustic forcing is not a prerequisite detour. Linear capillary perturbation in J03 and the arrest wave in J04 serve jet survival and impact prediction directly. Each chapter asks one brief synthesis after a comprehensive worked calculation; new chapters remain pending until understanding is demonstrated.

简谐声学驱动不是必修绕行。J03 的线性毛细扰动和 J04 的制动波直接服务于射流存续与冲击预测。每章在综合计算后要求一次简短综合回答；理解得到展示之前，新章保持待完成。
