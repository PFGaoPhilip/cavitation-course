The native N=100 model distributes flux in a 200 μm sphere and feeds back its mean pressure into one representative bubble state. Its initial void fraction is about 0.00187. The refined field deviates from that mean by up to 62.26 kPa and reaches −18.97 kPa absolute local water pressure. These results reject an unqualified physical uniform-population prediction from the common state. Local bubble states and secondary water cavitation require a different closure; the 16.65 kPa receiver output is a mathematical diagnostic.

原生 N=100 模型在半径 200 μm 球中分布通量，并将平均压力反馈给一个代表性气泡状态。初始空隙率约 0.00187。细化场相对均值的偏差最高为 62.26 kPa，局部水绝对压力最低为 −18.97 kPa。这些结果否定以共同状态给出无条件物理泡群预测。局部气泡状态及继发水空化需要其他闭合；16.65 kPa 接收输出属于数学诊断。

The 25 ns population baseline closed energy but failed the phase-constraint check. The authoritative finer time study is identified in the report and native file. This illustrates why one small conservation residual is not enough. Compare time, mesh and outer-domain evidence while holding the observer fixed.

25 ns 泡群基准虽闭合能量，却未通过相变约束检验。报告与原生文件标明权威的更细时间研究。此例说明一个很小的守恒残差并不足够。观测器保持固定，比较时间、网格与外域证据。

Apply Cauchy–Schwarz to the causal filter: ∫h²dt=1/(2τ_f). With the nearest receiver distance 0.4975 mm and E₀=67.712 nJ, the spherical progressive-wave bound is 318.16 kPa. It is not an attainable prediction and does not bound arbitrary directional focusing, a wall impact or a population beam. The largest sampled gas result is around 89.6 kPa; no continuous global optimization is claimed.

3 · Keep force paths and feasibility claims distinct / 区分受力路径与可行性结论

Droplet stamp: outer meniscus and internal collapse can coexist. Primary liquid layer: direct liquid→film and liquid→PVC→film require different receiver tractions. Embedded hydrogel: a sealed intact cavity supports pressure/deformation; an exterior liquid jet needs a specified channel, pocket or rupture. These two models verify source/pressure and embedded-equilibrium theories; film release, venting, jet topology and repeated reset remain unresolved branches.

液滴印章：外弯月面与内部塌陷机制可共存。主要液层：液体→薄膜与液体→PVC→薄膜需要不同接收牵引。嵌入水凝胶：完整密闭空腔支持压力／变形；外部液体射流需要明确通道、液袋或破裂。两模型核验源／压力及嵌入平衡理论；薄膜释放、排气、射流拓扑和重复复位仍待解决。

Comprehensive rebuild problem / 综合重建题

Reproduce the refined diagnostic population pressure, phase residuals and local feedback spread; explain why numerical convergence does not validate the common state. Calculate the spherical energy/response bound. Then halve the receiver bandwidth without changing its location, source or mesh: predict the bound and explain why the actual peak change must be recomputed. Conclude what can be reported for pressure, hydrogel embedding and repeatability.

重现细化诊断泡群压力、相变残差及局部反馈差异，解释为何数值收敛不能验证共同状态。计算球形能量／响应上界。随后在位置、源和网格不变时将接收带宽减半：预测上界，并说明为何实际峰值变化须重算。总结压力、水凝胶嵌入和重复性的可报告结论。

Halving f_c doubles τ_f; the upper bound decreases by 1/√2, from 318.16 to 224.97 kPa. Actual peak response depends on the waveform; it need not scale exactly this way. Report conditional reference pressures and equilibrium stretch classifications, the fixed-temperature radial instability, and the missing absorption/nucleation/rate-dependent failure/reset evidence. A jet speed and repeated intact-transfer limit are not outputs of these files.

f_c 减半使 τ_f 加倍；上界按 1/√2 下降，从 318.16 降为 224.97 kPa。实际峰值取决于波形，不一定严格按此比例缩放。报告有条件参考压力、平衡伸长分类、固定温度径向失稳，以及吸收／成核／速率失效／复位证据的缺口。这些文件不提供射流速度或重复完整转印界限。
