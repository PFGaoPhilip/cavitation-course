Why a bubble may not form

为何可能不会形成气泡

A pressure condition that would grow an existing bubble does not guarantee that a new bubble appears.

能够使已有气泡生长的压力条件，并不保证新气泡会出现。

历史课程／复习材料。当前学习顺序请使用完整教材课程。原第四、五章现为附录 A、B。

Our first two lessons began with a bubble already present. That is a different question from inception: where does the initial cavity come from? Even if vapor is favored by the local pressure, creating a new liquid–vapor surface costs energy.

前两课都从已有气泡出发。空化初生讨论的是另一个问题：最初的空腔从何而来？即使局部压力有利于蒸气存在，新建液–汽界面仍需要能量。

The small-embryo barrier

微小胚核的能垒

Consider an idealized spherical vapor embryo of radius r in a clean, isothermal liquid. Define the positive vapor-pressure advantage Δp using the equilibrium vapor pressure pv(T) and surrounding liquid pressure p∞. Classical nucleation theory then gives the reversible work of formation.

考虑洁净、等温液体中一个半径为 r 的理想化球形蒸气胚核。用平衡蒸气压 pv(T) 和周围液体压力 p∞ 定义正的蒸气压优势 Δp。经典成核理论据此给出可逆形成功。

Δp = pv(T) − p∞ > 0

ΔG(r) = 4πσr² − (4π/3)Δp r³.

The first term is the surface cost and grows as r². The second is the pressure-volume gain and grows as r³. The surface cost dominates a very small embryo; the volume gain can dominate once it becomes large enough.

第一项是表面能代价，随 r² 增长；第二项是压差带来的体积能收益，随 r³ 增长。胚核很小时，表面能代价占主导；胚核足够大时，体积能收益可以占主导。

r* = 2σ/Δp

This is the critical radius at the top of the barrier. In this model, an embryo smaller than r* tends to shrink; one larger than r* can grow if the favorable conditions persist. Lowering the surrounding pressure increases Δp and makes r* smaller.

这是能垒顶点处的临界半径。在该模型中，小于 r* 的胚核倾向于缩小；大于 r* 的胚核在有利条件持续时可以生长。降低周围液体压力会增大 Δp，从而减小 r*。

What this does and does not predict 这一模型能预测什么、不能预测什么

A trapped gas pocket already has an interface, while a particle or wall defect can provide an easier formation path. These cases do not simply use a smaller barrier in the same clean-liquid embryo formula. A very clean sample can still nucleate stochastically; the probability depends on pressure history, observation time, and volume. The equation above is an idealized energy picture, not a universal threshold for a shelled nanodroplet or a laser experiment.

被困气穴本身已有界面，而颗粒或壁面缺陷可能提供更容易的成核路径。这些情形不能简单地用同一个洁净液体胚核公式，只把能垒调低。非常洁净的样品仍可能随机成核；其概率取决于压力历程、观测时间和体积。上式是一幅理想化的能量图景，并非带壳纳米液滴或激光实验的普适阈值。

To see why one empty trial is inconclusive, imagine a constant nucleation rate J per unit volume per unit time in an observation volume V for duration t. An idealized independent-event model gives the probability of at least one event below.

要理解为什么一次未观察到事件的试验不能得出定论，设观测体积为 V、持续时间为 t，单位体积、单位时间的成核率恒为 J。理想化的独立事件模型给出如下至少发生一次事件的概率。

P(≥1) = 1 − exp(−JVt)

When J > 0, a finite pulse can still produce zero events. Repeating the experiment estimates an event frequency; a single non-event cannot distinguish a small probability from impossibility.

即使 J > 0，有限时长的脉冲仍可能没有事件。重复实验可以估计事件发生频率；单次未发生事件无法区分低概率与不可能。

One check

一道检查题

Twenty fresh clean samples receive identical short pressure pulses; two show cavitation. What does this say about the claim that the other eighteen were incapable of cavitating under that pulse?

二十份新的洁净样品受到相同的短时压力脉冲，其中两份出现空化。这对“另外十八份样品在该脉冲下不可能发生空化”的说法意味着什么？
